- **Graphviz / dot:** Used alongside `terraform graph | dot -Tsvg > graph.svg` to visually map out state dependencies.
- **GitHub Actions setup-terraform:** [hashicorp/setup-terraform](https://github.com/hashicorp/setup-terraform) - The official action for CI/CD pipelines.
